I am a PhD student at Harvard working with Yilun Du on vision and generative modelling. I also frequently collaborate closely with Ayush Tewari from University of Cambridge. I am currently interested in video generative modeling, robust 3D scene understanding, and robotic applications.

I graduated with my bachelor in Computer Science from the University of Delaware in 2024.
